## Tips for Store Listing Success

1. **Use the 79-character short description** - maximizes space while staying under limit
2. **Lead with benefits, not features** - focus on what users gain
3. **Include social proof** - mention user count, ratings, or testimonials
4. **Highlight the free trial** - reduces barrier to Premium conversion
5. **Use emojis strategically** - improves readability and catches attention
6. **Update regularly** - keep "What's New" section current with each release

Choose the description version that best matches your brand voice and target audience!
