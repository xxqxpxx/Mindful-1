# Awaytime Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [Date]

## Introduction

Welcome to Awaytime ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

**Our Privacy Philosophy:** We believe your screen time data is personal and should remain private. That's why we've designed Awaytime to work primarily with local data storage and minimal data collection.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly
- **App Selection:** The apps you choose to monitor (stored locally on your device)
- **Usage Limits:** Your daily time limits and goals (stored locally on your device)
- **Account Information:** If you create an account, we collect your email address for account management
- **Feedback:** Any feedback, support requests, or communications you send to us

### Information Collected Automatically
- **App Usage Data:** Time spent in monitored apps (processed and stored locally on your device)
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, app version
- **Analytics Data:** Basic app usage patterns to improve our service (anonymized and aggregated)
- **Crash Reports:** Technical information about app crashes to help us fix bugs

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal Content:** We never access the content of your apps, messages, photos, or files
- **Location Data:** We do not collect or track your location
- **Contact Information:** We do not access your contacts, calendar, or other personal data
- **Browsing History:** We do not track your web browsing or app content
- **Detailed Usage Patterns:** We do not create detailed profiles of your app usage behavior

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
- **Core Functionality:** To provide screen time tracking, app blocking, and goal management
- **Local Processing:** Most data processing happens on your device and never leaves it
- **Account Management:** To manage your account and subscription (if applicable)
- **Customer Support:** To respond to your questions and provide assistance

### Secondary Uses
- **App Improvement:** To understand how features are used and improve the app
- **Bug Fixes:** To identify and fix technical issues
- **Security:** To protect against fraud and ensure app security

### What We Do NOT Do
- **Sell Your Data:** We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information
- **Advertising:** We do not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes
- **Third-Party Sharing:** We do not share your usage data with third parties
- **Profiling:** We do not create detailed user profiles or behavioral analysis

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- **Usage Data:** Your app usage data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage
- **Settings:** Your preferences and settings are stored locally
- **Offline Functionality:** The app works fully offline without internet connection

### Cloud Storage (Optional)
- **Premium Sync:** Premium users can optionally enable cloud sync for settings backup
- **Encryption:** All cloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **User Control:** You can disable cloud sync at any time

### Security Measures
- **Encryption:** All sensitive data is encrypted using industry-standard methods
- **Access Controls:** Strict access controls limit who can access any data
- **Regular Audits:** We regularly review our security practices
- **Incident Response:** We have procedures in place to respond to any security incidents

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Share Information Only When:
- **Legal Requirements:** We are required by law, regulation, or court order
- **Safety Concerns:** To protect the safety of our users or the public
- **Business Transfer:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
- **With Your Consent:** When you explicitly agree to share information

### Service Providers
We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us operate the app:
- **Cloud Infrastructure:** For optional cloud sync functionality
- **Payment Processing:** For subscription management (they do not receive usage data)
- **Analytics Services:** For anonymized app performance metrics
- **Customer Support:** For providing user assistance

All service providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and data protection requirements.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Control
- **Access:** You can view all your data within the app
- **Export:** You can export your data in a standard format
- **Delete:** You can delete your data at any time
- **Correction:** You can correct any inaccurate information

### Privacy Settings
- **Analytics:** You can opt out of analytics data collection
- **Cloud Sync:** You can disable cloud synchronization
- **Notifications:** You can control notification preferences
- **Account Deletion:** You can delete your account and all associated data

### How to Exercise Your Rights
- **In-App Settings:** Most privacy controls are available in the app settings
- **Contact Us:** Email us at privacy@awaytime.app for assistance
- **Response Time:** We will respond to requests within 30 days

## Children's Privacy

Awaytime is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

For users between 13-18, we recommend parental guidance when using the app.

## International Data Transfers

If you are located outside the United States, please note that we may transfer your information to the United States for processing. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

## Data Retention

### Local Data
- **Usage Data:** Stored on your device until you delete the app or clear data
- **Settings:** Retained until you change them or delete the app

### Cloud Data (If Enabled)
- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
- **Backup Data:** Retained for 90 days after account deletion
- **Analytics Data:** Anonymized data may be retained for up to 2 years

### Deletion
- **Immediate:** You can delete your data immediately through app settings
- **Automatic:** Inactive accounts are automatically deleted after 2 years
- **Complete:** When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed

## Legal Basis for Processing (EU Users)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your information based on:
- **Contract Performance:** To provide the app services you've requested
- **Legitimate Interest:** To improve our app and provide customer support
- **Consent:** For optional features like analytics (which you can withdraw anytime)
- **Legal Obligation:** To comply with applicable laws and regulations

## California Privacy Rights

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- **Right to Know:** What personal information we collect and how we use it
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of your personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
- **Non-Discrimination:** We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@awaytime.app.
